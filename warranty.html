uPatch Warranty & Returns

At uPatch, we stand behind the quality of our products and want every customer to feel confident in their purchase. That’s why every uPatch Luggage Scale is backed by a 1-Year Limited Warranty.

What the Warranty Covers

This warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal, intended use for a period of one (1) year from the date of purchase.

  • LCD screen or display not functioning properly under normal conditions
  • Failure of the shake-to-charge mechanism
  • Strap or metal hook breaking under normal use within stated capacity limits
  • Manufacturing defects that affect accuracy or safe operation

What the Warranty Does Not Cover

  • Damage from misuse, abuse, accidents, or unauthorized modifications
  • Normal wear and tear, cosmetic damage, or scratches
  • Damage caused by exceeding the 110 lb / 50 kg weight capacity
  • Loss, theft, or improper storage conditions
  • Products purchased from unauthorized resellers

Warranty Claim Process

  1. Contact us at support@luggage-scale.com with proof of purchase and a description of the issue.
  2. Our team will review your request and provide troubleshooting steps or a return authorization.
  3. After inspection, defective items will be repaired or replaced at our discretion.
  4. In some cases, a refurbished replacement of equal or greater value may be provided.
