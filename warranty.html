uPatch Warranty & Returns

What the Warranty Covers

Every uPatch Luggage Scale is protected by a 1-Year Limited Warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship under normal, intended use.

  • LCD display or electronics malfunction
  • Shake-to-charge mechanism failure
  • Strap or hook breakage under normal load (≤110 lb / 50 kg)
  • Manufacturing defects affecting accuracy or safety

What the Warranty Does Not Cover

  • Misuse, abuse, accidents, or unauthorized repairs
  • Normal wear, cosmetic scratches, or aging
  • Overloading beyond rated capacity
  • Damage from improper storage or moisture
  • Purchases from non-authorized sellers

Warranty Claim Process

  1. Email support@luggage-scale.com with proof of purchase and issue details.
  2. Our team reviews and provides troubleshooting or return instructions.
  3. Defective units will be repaired or replaced at our discretion.
  4. Replacement may be a refurbished item of equal or greater value.

Customer Responsibilities

  • Proof of purchase is required for all claims.
  • Customer pays return shipping unless required by law.
  • Use a trackable service when sending returns.

This warranty gives you specific legal rights; you may have additional rights depending on your jurisdiction. uPatch is not liable for incidental or consequential damages.
