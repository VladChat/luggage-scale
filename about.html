About uPatch

Our Mission

At uPatch, our mission is to simplify travel while reducing electronic waste. We create practical tools that eliminate disposable batteries and make pre-flight prep easier for everyone.

The uPatch Luggage Scale is a battery-free travel scale powered by kinetic energy—just shake to activate. It’s designed for reliability, accuracy, and a lighter environmental footprint.

Our Story

Founded by Allyexporter LLC in Buffalo Grove, Illinois, uPatch started with a simple idea: travel accessories should work whenever you need them—no tiny coin cells, no leaks, no last-minute store runs. From early prototypes to the current model, we refine details with frequent travelers in mind.

We focus on clear usability: a durable strap and buckle, an easy-to-read display, and a dependable shake-to-charge mechanism you can trust at home or in the airport line.

What We Stand For

  • Sustainability — Battery-free operation means fewer disposables heading to landfills.
  • Reliability — Built for repeat use and consistent readings when technique is steady.
  • Practical Design — Compact, travel-proof, and easy to operate in real-world conditions.
  • Customer Care — Responsive support and clear documentation for every user.
