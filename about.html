About uPatch

Our Mission

At uPatch, our mission is to simplify travel while protecting the environment. We create innovative tools that eliminate the need for disposable batteries and minimize electronic waste.

The uPatch Luggage Scale is the world’s first battery-free travel scale powered by kinetic energy. Just shake it — and it’s ready to weigh. Durable, accurate, and built for travelers who value sustainability and simplicity.

Our Story

Founded by Allyexporter LLC in Buffalo Grove, Illinois, uPatch began with a small idea: make everyday travel tools smarter, cleaner, and longer-lasting. We believe that travel accessories should serve you for years — without constant maintenance or waste.

From concept to production, every uPatch product is designed with care, tested for durability, and refined to meet real traveler needs.

Our Values

  • Sustainability — Reducing battery waste through self-powered design.
  • Innovation — Using motion energy to power smart devices.
  • Reliability — Built for frequent travelers and long-term use.
  • Customer Care — Quick, personal support for every user.

Thank You for Supporting Smart, Sustainable Travel

Every uPatch scale purchased contributes to reducing electronic waste and promoting a cleaner planet. Thank you for being part of the change.
