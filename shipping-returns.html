Shipping & Returns

Shipping Information

All uPatch orders are shipped directly from our U.S. fulfillment partners. We offer fast domestic shipping with standard delivery times of 3–5 business days.

  • Orders are processed within 1–2 business days after purchase.
  • Tracking information is automatically emailed once your order ships.
  • We currently ship within the United States only.

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be completely satisfied with your uPatch product. If you are not happy with your purchase, you may return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or replacement.

  • Items must be unused and in their original packaging.
  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after receipt.
  • Customers are responsible for return shipping costs.

Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please contact our support team within 7 days of delivery. We’ll arrange for a prompt replacement or refund.

To initiate a return, contact us at support@luggage-scale.com.
