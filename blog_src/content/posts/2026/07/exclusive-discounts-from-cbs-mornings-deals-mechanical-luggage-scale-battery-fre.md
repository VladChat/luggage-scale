## Choosing a Reliable Travel Scale

A scale is a promise: tell me the truth, anywhere. The best ones make that promise simple.

Look for:

- A wide, easy-grip handle
- A strong, non-slip hook or strap
- Clear markings you can read in low light
- A body that won’t crack if you toss it in a tote
- A design that works in tight spaces like hotel hallways

Pro tip: Test at home. Weigh a bag on your bathroom scale, then hang it from your travel scale. Two readings within a pound is fine. Remember you’re aiming to avoid a fee line, not calibrate a lab.

Three quick checks before your next trip:

- Lift slowly: Quick jerks skew readings. Let the bag settle, then read.
- Balance the load: Center the hook and keep the bag off the floor.
- Recheck your heaviest bag: Repeat twice. Consistency matters more than perfection.
