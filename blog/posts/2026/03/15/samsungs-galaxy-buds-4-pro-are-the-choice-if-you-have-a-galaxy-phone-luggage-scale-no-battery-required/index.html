What’s new in the latest buds

Samsung didn’t just repaint the case and call it a day. The newest model brings together subtle hardware shifts and smarter tuning meant to make everyday listening cleaner, deeper, and more stable.

  • Upgraded woofers deliver stronger low-end without muddying mids.
  • Dual-amped hi‑fi tuning pushes clarity across the range.
  • A refined “blade” shape helps with mic placement and wind resistance.
  • Active noise cancellation gets a bump, especially in steady rumbles.
  • The case hinges and magnets feel tighter. Small touch, big daily win.

According to a The Verge review, the step up isn’t earth-shattering, but it’s meaningful. Think less reinvention, more refinement where it matters on the move: voice pickup in real air, isolation in real cabins, sound that holds together on noisy subways.

Sound that holds up in motion

Bass hits with more authority, but the bigger gain is control. Kick drums sound taut, not flabby. Vocals sit forward, not buried. If you bounce between podcasts and playlists, you’ll notice less EQ fiddling. You can leave profiles alone and still get balance.

Call clarity that earns trust

Mic arrays live and die by placement and processing. The reshaped exterior isn’t just for looks; it helps shield microphones from gusts and guides your voice where it needs to go. In practice, that means calls on a breezy corner don’t turn into a guessing game of “Can you hear me now?”

Noise cancellation that hushes the grind

Airplane cabins, HVAC hums, and bus engines present steady, low-frequency noise. That’s where the new model shows its best work. Cymbal shimmer in tracks stays intact while the drone around you gets gently tucked under. It’s a calmer way to listen.

Let’s be honest: these earbuds can pair with almost any phone, but they’re built to sing with Samsung’s own. If you carry a recent Galaxy device, you get the cleanest handshakes and the richest stream.

  • Fast Pair for frictionless first setup.
  • Higher-quality codec support within Samsung’s ecosystem for fuller sound.
  • Auto Switch among Galaxy tablets, watches, and phones when you bounce from gate changes to hotel TVs.
  • SmartThings Find when they wander into couch cushions or the plane seat pocket.

All of this translates to the travel day feeling smoother. You step from a rideshare into security, and the audio session survives network changes and app swaps. You open the case on the plane, and your phone doesn’t play tug-of-war with your tablet. In a world of tiny cuts, you’re spared a few.

Fit that respects long days

The “blade” shape is polarizing in photos and pleasing in practice. It gives your fingers a better purchase for quick adjustments. The no-wiggle seal helps ANC shine, and the weight distribution means fewer hot spots after hour two.

A better case is a better habit

The case snaps shut with conviction. Magnets guide the buds home, even when you’re fumbling at 2 a.m. in a dim hostel hallway. The more seamless it is to dock them, the more you’ll actually keep them charged.

Sound and silence in real places

Specs are theater. Airports are truth. Here’s how these buds held up across a whirlwind week through three cities and too many transit lines.

  • On the red-eye: Cabin drone softened to a far-off river. I could watch a movie at a sane volume. When the seatmate’s tray deployed mid-snack, the clatter didn’t yank me out of the scene.
  • In the metro: Announcements pierced the mix just enough on transparency mode. Music stayed intact; voices didn’t sound like they were beamed in from Mars.

  • In a café: Frother shriek dulled. Acoustic playlists felt present without the need to jam the buds deeper.

Battery life that respects layovers

Real use rarely mirrors lab numbers. With a mix of ANC on, calls, and streaming at medium volume, I made it through a full day—about 5 to 6 hours in-ear—and still had case top-ups to spare. Fast top-offs between flights made the difference: a brief charge while boarding gave me another hour.

Controls you won’t curse

Touch gestures have a reputation for false positives. Here, the sensitivity feels well-judged. Squeeze-and-hold brings up your go-to action. Double taps trigger tracks without phantom pauses when you brush hair aside or adjust a hat. The companion app lets you remap without a manual hunt.

Downsides and who should skip

No device is for everyone. A few trade-offs to weigh before you hit “buy.”

  • Price: At $249.99, they sit squarely in premium territory. If your budget caps lower, last year’s model or a solid midrange set might serve you nearly as well.
  • Ecosystem bias: You’ll get the best sound quality and features with a Galaxy phone. Pairing with iOS or other Android devices works, but you’ll lose a few flourishes.
  • Incremental upgrade: If you already own the prior generation and love it, the step up may feel subtle unless you crave stronger wind handling and steadier ANC.
  • The shape: The blade is ergonomic, but it’s a look. If you prefer an invisible bud, you may not love the aesthetic.

How they stack up

  • Against AirPods Pro 2: Apple still rules easy iPhone integration and spatial audio for Apple TV. Samsung edges noise in some steady-rumble scenarios and offers a richer stock EQ for many tracks.
  • Against Sony WF-1000XM5: Sony pushes deeper customization and superb ANC. Samsung’s fit feels more secure for quick movements and wins touch control reliability.
  • Against Pixel Buds Pro: Google plays nice with Android broadly. Samsung goes deeper with its own devices, and call clarity on breezy walks is improved.

Packing light tech: a traveler’s kit

Great earbuds are one piece of the puzzle. A smart, minimal kit keeps your day simple and your bag light. Here’s how I pack so the gear enables the trip, never dominates it.

  1. Build a charge habit
  • Top up while you shower or during the last 20 minutes before checkout.
  • Use a short USB‑C cable in a tiny zip pouch you can grab eyes‑closed.
  • Aim to end every night with both buds docked—even if you didn’t drain them.
  1. Tame the tangle
  • Assign a “tech pocket” in your daypack. Earbud case, cable, and power bank live there alone.
  • If you’re using a phone grip or wallet case, make sure it doesn’t block the case from laying flat on a wireless pad.
  1. Prep your sound before boarding
  • Download playlists and podcasts over Wi‑Fi. Streaming on a plane is misery.
  • Set a gentle EQ that works across genres. Bass boosts can get muddy against cabin hum.
  • Save one tap gesture for transparency mode. You’ll need it when the gate agent rattles off boarding zones.
  1. Keep calls sane in chaos
  • For street calls, angle your body slightly windward. The mic shields better when the gust hits the bud’s back.
  • Speak closer to your natural volume. Shouting only feeds the chaos.
  1. Respect your ears
  • If you’re using ANC for hours, intersperse 10-minute breaks. It’s not just about hearing health; comfort and awareness matter when you’re in motion in unfamiliar places.

The tiny tool that saves trips

There’s a moment every traveler knows: you reach the bag drop and feel a knot in your stomach. Did your souvenir spree push you over the weight limit? Are you about to pay a fee that costs more than what’s inside?

A simple fix belongs in the same pocket as your earbuds. A luggage scale no battery required sounds quaint until you need it. Mechanical models with a spring and a hook won’t die on you at 4 a.m. in a guesthouse with one wall outlet and three devices already charging.

Why it helps:

  • Reliability: No battery anxiety. No half-drained cells worsening accuracy.
  • Simplicity: Clip, lift, read. Even groggy, you get a clear number.
  • Durability: Fewer electronics mean less to break when your bag takes a tumble.

Pair it with your audio routine. While your earbuds top up before a taxi, take 60 seconds to weigh your case. Shift a hoodie to your personal item if you’re close to the limit. That tiny check can save an argument at the counter, a repack on the floor, and a fee that sours the day.

How to use it well

  • Weigh at your departure lodging, not just the airport. You’ll catch overages when you still have time to rearrange.
  • Calibrate with a known item once. A 1L water bottle weighs about 1 kilogram when full; use it to sanity-check your scale.
  • Keep it on the outside of your backpack in a quick-grab pocket. If it’s buried, you won’t use it.

Q: Are they comfortable for long wear? A: The refined shape spreads contact points well, reducing pressure spots. With the right ear tip size and a proper seal, most users can wear them for multi-hour stretches without fatigue.
