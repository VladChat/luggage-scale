How to Measure Accurately

  • Charge first: give the scale a few firm shakes until the screen lights up.
  • Hook correctly: loop the strap through a sturdy handle and confirm the buckle is fully engaged.
  • Lift smoothly: raise the bag in one motion and hold steady for 2–3 seconds while the reading stabilizes.
  • Mind the units: switch to lb or kg to match your airline’s format.
  • Double-check: if you’re near the limit, weigh twice and use the average for peace of mind.
